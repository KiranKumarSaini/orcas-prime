## The costs nobody itemises

Most "Shopify costs in India" answers quote the subscription and stop. The subscription is usually the smallest line. Here is the whole thing.

## Recurring monthly costs

| Item | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Shopify Basic subscription | ~₹1,500–₹2,000 |
| GST on subscription (18%) | ~₹270–₹360 |
| Apps (3–4 essential) | ₹500–₹2,500 |
| Domain (amortised annually) | ~₹100 |
| Payment gateway fees | ~2% of revenue |
| Shipping aggregator | Per-shipment, varies |

A small Basic-plan store typically runs **₹3,500–₹8,000 a month all-in**, before shipping and before ad spend. The range is wide because app choices vary enormously between stores.

## Transaction fees, which are easy to miss

Two separate things get confused here:

- **Payment gateway charges** — roughly 2% of order value in India, charged by Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU or whoever processes the card
- **Shopify's third-party transaction fee** — an additional charge Shopify levies if you use an external gateway rather than Shopify Payments, around 2% on the Basic plan

If you are on an external gateway you may be paying both. At ₹5,00,000 monthly revenue, a 2% differential is ₹10,000 a month, which is more than the entire rest of your platform stack. Worth checking carefully before you commit to a gateway.

## One-time build cost

This is where quotes vary wildly, and the variation is real rather than arbitrary.

| Approach | Range | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Do it yourself | ₹0 | A free theme, your own time, a functional store |
| Freelancer | ₹8,000–₹25,000 | Theme setup, catalogue loaded, basic integrations |
| Agency build | ₹10,000–₹50,000 | The above plus brand pass, checkout testing, SEO basics, handoff |
| Custom development | ₹1,00,000+ | Bespoke design and functionality |

**Orcas Prime charges ₹10,000–₹20,000** for a store build, depending on scope. That covers theme setup and brand pass, your first fifty SKUs loaded, payment and shipping integration tested end to end, checkout and cart, on-page SEO basics, and a walkthrough of the admin so you can run it yourself if you want to.

## What actually deserves your money

If your budget is limited, spend it in this order:

1. **Product photography.** It affects conversion more than everything else on this page combined. A great store with poor images converts badly; a plain store with excellent images converts fine.
2. **Payment and shipping integration done properly.** A checkout that fails silently costs you every order it touches, and most sellers do not find out for weeks.
3. **The build.** Getting the structure right early saves rebuilding later.
4. **A paid theme.** Last. Often never.

## The cost that dwarfs all of this

None of the above is the real number. **Traffic is the real number.** A Shopify store with no visitors generates nothing regardless of how well it is built, and in India the realistic starting ad budget is ₹1,000–₹2,000 a day.

Over a month that is ₹30,000–₹60,000 — several times the entire platform and build cost combined. Any plan that budgets for the store but not for traffic has budgeted for the wrong thing.
