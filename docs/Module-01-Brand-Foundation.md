## How To Use This Document

This module answers **who Orcas Prime is**. It does not describe the website —
that is Module 3. It does not describe the buyer — that is Module 2.

When a later decision anywhere in the project contradicts something here, this
document wins. These twenty decisions are the constitution.

Every decision has a permanent ID. Reference decisions by ID rather than
restating them.

## Part I — Positioning

### M1-01 · Orcas Prime is not positioned as an agency

Deliberately rejected: Performance Marketing Agency, Shopify Agency, Meta Ads
Agency, Full-Service Digital Agency.

These describe *activities*. Orcas Prime is defined by what it takes
responsibility for, not by which platforms it operates. Any positioning that
names a channel is a positioning that expires when that channel does.

### M1-02 · Orcas Prime sells ownership, not execution

Execution is expected. Ownership is remembered.

Every capability exists only because it supports ownership. Meta Ads are a
tool. Google Ads are a tool. Shopify is a tool. The product being sold is that
someone else now carries responsibility for the growth of the business.

### M1-03 · We exist for builders, not marketers

The founder Orcas Prime admires is obsessed with building a better product.
They spend their time on quality, packaging, customer experience, long-term
brand value.

They are not experts in Meta Ads. They are not expected to be. That is exactly
why Orcas Prime exists.

**Consequence:** the site never assumes marketing literacy. See Module 6 §2.

## Part II — The Moat

### M1-04 · The internal moat is Decision Quality at Scale

Orcas Prime should not become a company that executes requests. It should
become a company that consistently makes better ecommerce decisions than the
average founder or agency.

Execution is necessary. Better judgement is what creates long-term value.

### M1-05 · Orcas Prime is a decision company that executes

Execution is the visible output. Decision-making is the real product.

No department optimises for activity. Every department optimises for better
business decisions. This governs hiring — recruit good business thinkers who
happen to know Meta, not "Meta Ads Experts."

### M1-06 · Everything learned becomes reusable

Knowledge never stays inside one person's head. Knowledge from one brand
improves every future brand.

This creates a compounding advantage competitors cannot easily copy. The
mechanism is the Growth Decision Log — see Module 5 §3.

## Part III — How We Communicate

### M1-07 · Teach before selling

The objective is not to convince founders that Orcas Prime is smart. It is to
help founders make better decisions.

If the content genuinely improves a founder's thinking, trust is created before
the first sales conversation.

### M1-08 · Demonstrate intelligence, never claim it

Never write "we are experts," "industry-leading agency," "best ecommerce
company." These create scepticism.

Instead demonstrate thinking — explain a real decision and the reasoning behind
it. See Module 3 §5, Module 7 §3.

### M1-09 · The website is a risk reduction machine

Most agency websites try to increase excitement. This one reduces uncertainty.

A founder arriving carries dozens of hidden objections. The site's job is to
remove them, one at a time. See Module 5 §7 for the full objection map.

### M1-10 · Every page must have a job

No page exists because other agency websites have it. Every page answers one
important founder question.

Homepage: *can these people actually solve my problem?*
Pricing: *why does this fee structure exist?*
Work: *has this worked before?*
Guides: *do they know more than I do?*

## Part IV — Ideology

### M1-11 · Great products deserve to win

The deepest belief behind Orcas Prime.

Not every ecommerce business — founders who have built genuinely good products
but struggle to build the ecommerce machine around them.

This is the test for every future decision: *does this help great products win?*

### M1-12 · Publish thinking, not marketing

The content strategy is not company news, generic blogs, trend articles, or
thin SEO content.

It is the reasoning behind real decisions. See Module 7.

---

## Part V — The Website's Nature

### M1-13 · The website is not a sales machine, it is a trust machine

A founder rarely arrives thinking *"I can't wait to hire an agency."* They
arrive carrying uncertainty.

The site's function is to reduce that uncertainty, not to manufacture desire.

### M1-14 · We remove objections, we do not manufacture excitement

Explicitly rejected: urgency, scarcity, limited slots, countdown timers,
exit-intent popups, "book now."

See Module 6 §6 for the full banned-CTA list.

### M1-15 · Orcas Prime has two brands — not two companies, two layers

**External brand:** what founders experience — ownership, trust, partnership,
execution.

**Internal brand:** how the company actually operates — the decision engine,
the accumulated knowledge, the judgement.

The external brand earns the client. The internal brand is why the work is
good. Most of the internal brand is never marketed; it is simply true.

## Part VI — Why We Win

### M1-16 · Compete on thinking, not execution

Execution is expected. Thinking is rare.

Most agencies proudly explain what they do. Very few explain why they made a
particular recommendation.

### M1-17 · Publish thinking instead of marketing

Traditional agencies publish service pages, company updates, award
announcements.

Orcas Prime publishes reasoning: how a decision was made, what alternatives
existed, why they were rejected.

*(Closely related to M1-12; retained separately because the original document
locked both. M1-12 sets the content strategy; M1-17 sets what gets published.)*

### M1-18 · The website should feel like publishing, not advertising

Visitors should feel they are learning from experienced operators rather than
being sold to by marketers.

Every important page leaves the founder with at least one genuinely useful
insight — even if they never become a Partner Brand.

### M1-19 · Demonstrate judgement publicly

Wherever possible, explain: why we recommended something, what alternatives we
considered, what risks existed, why we rejected other options.

This is the public-facing half of M1-06. Mechanism specified in Module 5 §3.

### M1-20 · Every page must change how the founder thinks

The objective is not to inform. It is transformation.

A founder begins thinking *"I need someone to run my ads."* They should end
thinking *"I need someone to own the growth of my business."*
