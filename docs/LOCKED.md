## 1. Ideology & Belief (the top of the pyramid)

**Ideology — Decision #49 (LOCKED):**
> Great products deserve to win.

Not the biggest budgets. Not the loudest marketers. Not the most agencies.
This is the test for every future decision: *does this help great products win?*

**Core belief (chosen by Kiran over 5 other options, including ChatGPT's own favorite):**
> The future of ecommerce agencies is not selling services, it's taking ownership.

**Brand promise — Decision #1 (LOCKED):**
> We take ownership of your ecommerce growth.

Not "we run ads," "we build stores," "we manage marketplaces" — those are
capabilities. Ownership is the promise.

**Company philosophy (the internal operating truth):**
> You build the product. We build the business around it.

**North Star (two-layer — outcome and mechanism):**
> Help great products win. *(the deeper North Star)*
> Founder Peace of Mind. *(the outcome that signals we're succeeding)*

## 3. The Trust Ladder (use for proof/testimonial section ordering)

| Level | Founder's question | Who answers it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Can these people do the work? | Every agency |
| 2 | Can I trust them? | Some |
| 3 | Will they care as much as I do? | Very few |
| 4 | **Will they make better decisions than I would?** | **Where Orcas Prime lives** |

> We don't expect you to trust us on day one. Our job is to earn that trust with every decision we make.

## 4. Emotional Target & Tone Rules

**The one emotion the homepage creates — Decision #12 (LOCKED):**
> Relief. Not excitement, not urgency.
> "Finally, I don't have to solve this alone anymore."

**Never say this:**
> "You shouldn't have to think about this anymore."
> "You can finally focus on your product."

(Both logged as candidate relief-lines — use sparingly, don't overuse "finally.")

**Founder is the hero, never Orcas Prime — Decision #5 (LOCKED):**
> Orcas Prime is never the hero. The founder is.

**Voice rules (all LOCKED):**
- Never "Contact Us" — always the next logical step (Decision #35). Approved CTA: *Discuss Your Brand* / *Let's Evaluate Your Business* / *See If We're The Right Fit*.
- Never sound desperate — no "limited slots," "hurry," "book now" (Decision #36).
- No "clients" — "Partner Brands" (Decision #37).
- Prove thinking, not experience — never "10 years, 500 clients" (Decision #38).
- Publish thinking, feel like publishing not marketing (Decision #39).
- Every page has an opinion — not "Meta Ads Management" but "Why most ecommerce brands start Meta Ads too early" (Decision #40).
